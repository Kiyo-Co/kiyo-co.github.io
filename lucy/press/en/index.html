What it does.

Say it, done

“Open Discord”, “game volume 30”, “take a screenshot”. Understands natural speech, not memorized phrases.

Hears you offline

Her name and your commands are recognized right on the PC. Your voice never leaves it.

For gaming

Voice macros with per-game profiles: say “shield” — keys pressed. Sound, windows, OBS and Discord without alt-tabbing.

Scenarios

Open, wait, press, switch audio, open a link — in one phrase, no code.

AI of your choice

Cloud models with your own key, or a local AI right on your PC.

On-screen character

Shows when Lucy is listening, thinking and answering.

Music by voice

Spotify and Yandex Music: “play my favorites”, “next track” — without opening the player.

Ready-made text.

Use it whole or in parts.

Lucy is a voice assistant for Windows by Kiyo Studio, a small independent studio. Say “Lucy” and she opens apps, changes the volume, manages windows, presses keys in your game or answers with AI.

What sets her apart: listening happens right on your computer, offline. Your voice never leaves the PC, and Lucy hears you even over games and music. Gamers get voice macros and scenarios; everyone gets quick commands, music by voice, timers, weather, a phone remote and a live on-screen character.

Lucy is coming soon to Steam Early Access.
