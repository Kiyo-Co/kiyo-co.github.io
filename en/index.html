FAQ

Does it need internet?

No. Lucy hears her name and your commands on the PC itself. Internet is only needed for cloud AI, weather and music services.

Which languages?

English and Russian: commands, interface and Lucy’s voice. You pick the language on first launch.

Will it get in the way of games?

When it’s quiet, Lucy barely touches the CPU, and the GPU is left to your game by default. For macros in games with anti-cheat, check the game’s rules.

Where does my data go?

Your voice stays on your PC. Details are in the privacy policy.
